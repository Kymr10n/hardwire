# Code of Conduct

Hardwire follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). In short: be welcoming, be respectful, assume good faith, and keep discussions about the work.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainer, [@Kymr10n](https://github.com/Kymr10n), privately through GitHub. Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove comments, commits or issues that break this code, and may block repeat offenders, following the Covenant's enforcement guidelines.
